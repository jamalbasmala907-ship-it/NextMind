Market Friction & Problem Statement

The Driver's Dilemma: Fragmented Mobility

Modern commuters and fleet operators are forced into multiple siloed apps, unpredictable queue wait times, and broken physical terminals.

📱

1. App Fragmentation

Drivers juggle between 4 to 6 separate applications: gas station finders, EV charging networks (EV-Edge, Cello, Gnrgy), toll passes, and credit card terminals.

Result: Cluttered smartphone & fragmented receipts
⏳

2. Queue & Status Blindness

EV owners arrive at destination charging poles only to find them occupied or defective. ICE drivers face peak-hour queues with zero advance visibility.

Result: 18% average route detours & wasted dwell time
💳

3. Payment & Invoice Friction

Damaged outdoor POS terminals, incompatible RFID keyfobs, and missing paper receipts complicate tax reporting for private motorists and enterprise fleets.

Result: Lost VAT recovery & manual expense logging

Tailored for Every Energy Persona

⛽

Private ICE Drivers

EV Commuters

Pain Point: Range anxiety, broken plugs, opaque kWh tariffs.
Fuel&Go Value: Real-time socket status (CCS DC 150kW+), transparent price breakdown, and integrated battery % target calculation.

Goal: Guaranteed charging bay availability with predictable costs
🚛

Fleet & Enterprise

Pain Point: Managing mixed diesel/EV fleet receipts across multiple drivers.
Fuel&Go Value: Consolidated monthly tax invoicing, spending caps per vehicle, and automated ERP data export.

Goal: 100% digital tax audit compliance and zero expense headaches
The Solution

Fuel&Go: One Tap, Any Energy

A unified, cross-energy platform connecting smart stations, IoT pump controllers, and mobile drivers into a single continuous journey.

🗺️

Unified Map

Petrol pumps and high-speed EV chargers visualized side-by-side with live power and status flags.

📡

IoT Telemetry

Direct station connectivity reporting live socket power, connector types (CCS / Type 2), and queue wait times.

📲

Remote Activation

Authorize the pump or unlock the EV plug directly from the smartphone with precision geofencing.

🧾

Instant Pay

Biometric Apple/Google Pay, unified corporate billing, and automated bilingual digital PDF receipts.

  • 🔹 Unified Discovery Engine: Station network filterable by energy grade, plug type & amenities.
  • 🔹 Dual Interactive Flows: Fueling vs. EV charging interactive execution simulator.
  • 🔹 Python-Powered Calculation Engine: Real-time financial estimator with BiDi Israeli Shekel (₪) support.
  • 🔹 Three.js 3D Visualizer: Stylized vehicle & station workflow spatial animation.
  • 🔹 Bilingual Digital Tax Receipt: Compliant VAT breakdown in Hebrew (כולל מע"מ) and English.

🔮 Production Roadmap (Post-MVP)

  • 🔸 Native IoT Pump Hardware API: Hardware handshake via OCPP 2.0.1 and IFSF industrial protocols.
  • 🔸 Smart AI Route Optimization: Multi-stop EV charging route planner factoring elevation, temperature & traffic.
  • 🔸 Fleet ERP Integration: Direct sync with SAP, Priority, and corporate expense management systems.
  • 🔸 C-Store Click & Collect: Order coffee and convenience store snacks while charging.
  • 🔸 Predictive Dynamic Surge Pricing: Dynamic tariff recommendations based on grid demand.
